# AI Glossary

## Artificial Intelligence Glossary

A practical reference for the most important terms in artificial intelligence.

---

## A

### AI Agent

An AI system capable of performing tasks, making decisions, and interacting with tools or external systems with varying degrees of autonomy.

### AI Alignment

The field concerned with making AI systems behave according to intended goals and values.

---

## E

### Embedding

A numerical representation of data such as text, images, or other information that captures relationships and semantic characteristics.

---

## F

### Fine-Tuning

The process of adapting a pretrained model to a specific task, domain, or dataset.

---

## L

### Large Language Model

A machine-learning model trained on large amounts of data to process and generate language.

---

## R

### RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with generative AI so that a model can use external information when generating an answer.

---

## T

### Transformer

A neural-network architecture that uses attention mechanisms and forms the foundation of many modern language and multimodal models.

---

## V

### Vector Database

A database designed to store and search numerical vector representations, commonly used in semantic search and RAG systems.
