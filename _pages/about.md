# About AI Intelligence Daily

## AI News. Tools. Research. Explained.

AI Intelligence Daily is an independent AI publication focused on helping readers understand the rapidly changing world of artificial intelligence.

We cover technology from three perspectives:

### News

What happened?

### Technology

How does it work?

### Impact

Why does it matter?
